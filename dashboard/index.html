Methodology

fitLaunchValue — ridge log10 fit on 93 cleaned traded protocols (93 kept, 51 thrown out: bad caps, extreme mcap/tvl and one MAD residual pass): log10(mcap) from log10(tvl), clamped 7d growth and log10(age). Every board value comes from that one fit, multiplied by category token odds and capped at $2T.

walkForward validation — fit on the earlier three quarters, score the held-out quarter: median APE —. leaveOneOut validation — refit without each sampled protocol: median APE —, RMSLE — log10 units. All three measured errors travel on every row.
